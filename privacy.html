Recipes.Ai Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Recipes.Ai ("the app") is an iPhone app for saving and organizing recipes. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: your recipes stay on your iPhone, there is no Recipes.Ai server, and the developer never receives your recipes or account details.

Information stored on your device

Recipes, photos, folders and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Screenshots you choose to import are read on the device (using Apple's on-device text recognition) and are not uploaded.

Sign-in and backups

You can optionally sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft (Outlook) to back up your recipes to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive. The app sends your backup file directly to the service you choose, using your own account. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone's secure Keychain. The developer has no access to your accounts or backups. Those services' own privacy policies apply: Apple, Google, Microsoft.

Importing recipes and searching

When you import a link or search for recipes, the app connects directly to that website (for example Instagram, Facebook, a recipe blog, or Google Search) to read the recipe. Those websites may receive the usual information any browser sends, such as your IP address.

Claude AI features (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Claude features in Settings:

  • With your own API key: the recipe text or question you send is transmitted directly to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) using your key. Your key is stored in the iPhone's Keychain.
  • With the Claude app: Recipes.Ai opens Claude with your request, and you choose what to paste back.

See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads load, and on all iPhones it asks for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before using your advertising identifier for personalised ads. You can change your ad choices at any time in Settings → Premium → Ad Privacy Choices. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Recipes.Ai Premium (a one-time purchase) and the paid version show no ads and load no advertising code.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app only learns whether Premium is unlocked; it never sees your payment details.

Analytics

The app has no analytics or tracking of its own.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting a recipe or deleting the app removes the data from your iPhone. Backups you made are in your own iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive and can be deleted there. Signing out in the app removes the stored sign-in tokens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
